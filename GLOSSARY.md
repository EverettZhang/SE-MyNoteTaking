# Notes

MyNoteTaking lets people write and organize personal notes.

## Language

**Note**:
A saved piece of writing with a title and content.

**Deleted note**:
A note removed from the normal collection while its original record remains available for restoration. It may later be permanently removed under the retention rule.
_Avoid_: Permanently deleted note

**Restore**:
Return a deleted note to the normal collection with its identity and contents intact.
_Avoid_: Re-create

**Undo Delete**:
The immediate option to restore a note after deleting it.
